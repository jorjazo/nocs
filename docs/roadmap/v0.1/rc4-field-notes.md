| 22:00 | The box's access point, outdoors | Neither a laptop nor a phone could join `NOCS-xxxx` from about a metre away: the laptop saw the network now and then, the phone always, and neither completed joining (the same as on rc2's night). **The two USB 3 devices on the Pi were the cause:** with them unplugged both joined every time, whatever the cables tried.
