# 0001. Record architecture decisions

Date: 2026-10-27 · Status: Accepted

## Context

NOCS is developed by a single maintainer in limited weekly time, with long
gaps between sessions. Decisions and their reasons are easily forgotten.

## Decision

Record significant decisions as short ADRs in `docs/adr/`, numbered
sequentially. Accepted ADRs are not edited; they are superseded by new ones.

## Consequences

Decisions are cheap to look up and to revisit deliberately. A small writing
cost per decision.
